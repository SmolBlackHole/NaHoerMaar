Nuxt Fonts may bundle the configured families below in a production build. The
families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The
family-specific OFL text and copyright attribution are maintained by Google
Fonts in each linked directory:

- DM Sans: [local OFL notice](fonts/dmsans.txt), [source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans)
- Geist: [local OFL notice](fonts/geist.txt), [source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/geist)
- Inter: [local OFL notice](fonts/inter.txt), [source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/inter)
- Outfit: [local OFL notice](fonts/outfit.txt), [source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/outfit)
- Public Sans: [local OFL notice](fonts/publicsans.txt), [source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/publicsans)
- Poppins: [local OFL notice](fonts/poppins.txt), [source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/poppins)
- Raleway: [local OFL notice](fonts/raleway.txt), [source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/raleway)

The local files contain the complete family-specific license text. The license
FAQ is available at https://scripts.sil.org/OFL.
